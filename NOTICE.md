# Notice

## What this repository claims

This repository **does not claim ownership** of the hadith texts, the section titles, the references or the grades it contains. It only repackages data published by [fawazahmed0/hadith-api](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api) so that an app can download it in compact verified files.

## What is known about the rights

- `hadith-api` is released under **The Unlicense** (see `upstream/LICENSE`). That licence covers what its author owns; it cannot grant rights that belong to others.
- According to the upstream maintainer (issue #8), the Arabic and English texts come from sunnah.com, other translations from the websites in `upstream/References.md`, and the grades from al-maktaba.org. No permission from those sites is documented upstream, and the question about the provenance of the Arabic text (issue #138) is unanswered at the time of writing.
- The Arabic texts are the words of the Prophet ﷺ and of authors who died centuries ago. What may carry rights is the digital edition (diacritics, punctuation, formatting, compilation).
- Translations are **not published here**. They were left out on purpose until the source and the right to redistribute each one has been checked.
- Grades are the published judgements of the named scholars and are attributed to them.

## No warranty

The data is provided as is. It is a study aid and not a substitute for the printed critical editions or for a scholar; verify before relying on any text, number or grade.
