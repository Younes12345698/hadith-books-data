# Requesting removal or a correction

If you own rights in anything published here, or you find an error:

1. Open an issue in this repository with the edition id (for example `ara-bukhari`), what should be removed or corrected, and, for a rights claim, how you hold the rights.
2. Content covered by a credible rights claim is removed from `data/v1` as soon as it is read, without waiting for a discussion, and `index.json` is republished so that the app stops offering it. Copies already downloaded to a device are deleted when the app next refreshes its catalogue.
3. Data errors are fixed upstream where possible, and a corrected file is published under a new hash.

لأي صاحب حق أو من يجد خطأً: افتح Issue في هذا المستودع مع معرّف الإصدار ووصف الطلب. تُسحب المادة المشمولة بادعاء حق معقول فور الاطّلاع عليه.
